1. Introduction

Welcome to the UG Telecaller mobile application, operated by Unais Group.

We are committed to protecting your personal information and respecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains in simple and transparent terms how we collect, use, store, and safeguard your data when you use the UG Telecaller app.

2. Information We Collect

To provide our customer relationship management, inquiry tracking, and administrative services, we may collect the following types of information:

User Profile Information

Your name, email address, mobile number, username, assigned role (e.g., Telecaller, Team Leader, Branch Head, Sale Head, Admin), and login credentials.

Customer & Lead Information

Client name, contact numbers, admission enquiry details, meeting schedules, notes, and inquiry status updates entered by authorized users.

Activity & Performance Data

Attendance logs, daily task reports, leave requests, and performance metrics.

Device & Technical Information

Device model, operating system version, app version, network status, and error/crash reports for app maintenance.

3. Permissions We Use & Why

Our app requests minimal device permissions only to enable essential functions:

Internet Access

Necessary for connecting securely with our central database and synchronizing your daily work, records, and reports.

Storage

Allows you to export and download daily reports, admission lists, and Excel summary documents to your device.

You can manage applicable device permissions at any time through your device settings.

4. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information for clear and legitimate business purposes:

Lead & Admission Tracking

To manage lead assignments, follow-ups, and admission tracking efficiently.

Reports & Analytics

To generate daily activity reports, summaries, and performance calculations.

Attendance & Leave Management

To track attendance, work hours, and process team leave requests.

Security & Account Protection

To maintain account security, verify identity, and prevent unauthorized access.

Technical Diagnostics & Support

To diagnose software issues, improve app performance, and provide technical support.

5. Data Sharing & Disclosure

We value your trust and keep your data confidential:

No Commercial Sale

We DO NOT sell, rent, trade, or distribute your personal data or customer information to third-party marketers or advertisers under any circumstance.

Internal Access

Information is accessible only to authorized staff and managers within Unais Group based on their assigned roles.

Secure Cloud Hosting

Data is securely stored on protected servers with encryption and strict access controls.

Legal Requirements

We may disclose information only if required by applicable law, regulation, or legal process.

6. Data Security

We take necessary security measures to keep your data safe and protected against unauthorized access:

Secure Connection

All information is safely transmitted using standard encryption protocols.

Protected Access

Only authorized staff have access to relevant work data based on defined privileges.

Account Safety

Login credentials and data are stored safely on protected servers with strict security.

7. Data Retention & Account Deletion

We retain your account data for as long as your account is active with Unais Group or as needed to comply with business and legal obligations.

Account & Data Deletion:

You have the right to request deletion of your account and associated personal data at any time. To request data or account deletion, please contact your administrator or reach out via our support email button below. We will process your request promptly.

8. Children's Privacy

The UG Telecaller app is an enterprise business application designed for workplace staff and authorized professionals. We do not knowingly collect personal data from individuals under 18 years of age.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When changes are made, we will update the "Last Updated" date at the top of this page. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.
